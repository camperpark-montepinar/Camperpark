2. Purpose of the Processing of Personal Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Managing stay bookings and the associated payments.

  • Managing online check-in and complying with the legal obligation to keep records and report traveller information to the Spanish Ministry of the Interior (Royal Decree 933/2021).

  • Sending communications related to your booking and stay.

  • Answering the enquiries you send us.

  • Complying with applicable tax, accounting and security obligations.

2.1. Data Retention Period

We will keep your personal data from the moment you give us your consent until you withdraw it or request restriction of processing. In such cases, we will keep your data blocked for the legally required periods.

However, traveller data collected at check-in will be kept for three years from the end of the service, in accordance with traveller registration regulations, and billing data will be kept for the applicable tax and commercial periods. After those periods, the data will be deleted.

3. Legal Basis and Data Collected

The legal basis for processing your data is the express consent given through a positive, affirmative act (filling in the relevant form and ticking the box accepting this policy) when you provide us with your personal data.

In addition, the processing of your data is based on the performance of the booking contract you request and on compliance with legal obligations, in particular Royal Decree 933/2021 on the keeping of records and reporting of traveller information.
