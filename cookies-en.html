General information

Following the amendment of the Spanish “Information Society Services Law” (LSSICE) introduced by Royal Decree-Law 13/2012, it is mandatory to obtain the express consent of users of all websites that use non-essential cookies, before they browse them.

What are cookies?

Cookies and other similar technologies such as local shared objects, flash cookies or pixels are tools used by web servers to store and retrieve information about their visitors, as well as to ensure the correct operation of the site.

These devices allow the web server to remember certain data about the user, such as their preferences for viewing the pages of that server, name and password, products of most interest, etc.

Cookies affected by the regulations and exempt cookies

According to the EU directive, the cookies that require the user’s informed consent are analytics cookies and advertising and affiliation cookies. Technical cookies and those necessary for the operation of the website or for providing services expressly requested by the user are exempt.

What types of cookies are there?

By ownership

First-party cookies: those sent to the user’s terminal from a computer or domain managed by the publisher itself and from which the service requested by the user is provided.

Third-party cookies: those sent to the user’s terminal from a computer or domain that is not managed by the publisher but by another entity that processes the data obtained through the cookies.

By duration

Session cookies: a type of cookie designed to collect and store data while the user accesses a web page.

Persistent cookies: a type of cookie in which the data remains stored on the terminal and can be accessed and processed for a period defined by the party responsible for the cookie, which can range from a few minutes to several years.

By function

Necessary cookies: those essential for the correct operation of the website. They do not collect any user data and store information such as language preference, whether the cookie policy has been accepted, etc.

Analytics cookies: those that, whether processed by us or by third parties, allow us to quantify the number of users and thus carry out the measurement and statistical analysis of how users use the service offered. To do this, browsing on our website is analysed in order to improve the products or services we offer.

Advertising or marketing cookies: those that allow the most effective possible management of the advertising spaces that the publisher may have included on a web page, application or platform from which the requested service is provided, based on criteria such as the edited content or the frequency with which ads are shown.

Behavioural advertising or personalised marketing cookies: those that allow the most effective possible management of the advertising spaces that the publisher may have included. These cookies store information about users’ behaviour obtained through continuous observation of their browsing habits, which makes it possible to develop a specific profile to show advertising based on it. It is also possible that, when visiting a web page or opening an email containing an ad or promotion for our products or services, a cookie is installed in your browser that allows us to later show you advertising related to your search, and to monitor our ads, for example the number of times they are viewed, where they appear, at what time they are seen, etc.
